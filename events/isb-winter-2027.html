The four days

Four days, from an idea to a demo.

Short lessons each morning, then project time with our team beside every student.

  1. Day 01Choose a project

    Students pick something they care about and write a one-page plan.

  2. Day 02Talk to people, make a first version

    Interviews with the people it is for, then a first working version.

  3. Day 03Test, improve, and design

    Classmates try it. Students decide what to fix and cut, then name and style it.

  4. Day 04Launch and present

    The project goes live at its own link, and each student presents it.

Does my child need to know how to code?+

No. Students use AI coding tools to make their project, learn how it works, and make every decision along the way.

What kind of project can they make?+

Anything they care about: a study tool, a club or cause website, a game, or a family tool. Day one helps them choose.

Is using AI just cheating?+

No. AI is a helper. Students make the decisions and, on the last day, explain how their project works and why.

How is this different from the Entrepreneurship & AI Intensive?+

It is shorter and starts from a personal interest, not a startup idea. The five-day Intensive covers the full path from idea to pitch.

What happens after the four days?+

Students keep the project, its code, and its link. To keep going, ask about our Year-Long Project Mentorship.
