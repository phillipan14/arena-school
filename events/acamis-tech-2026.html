From Lab to Classroom: Running an AI Student Program Inside a School

A 60-minute hands-on workshop for teachers and tech coaches, built on a case study from the International School of Beijing.

  • WhenSunday, October 18
  • Time11:20 am–12:20 pm (per the agenda)
  • WhereKeystone Academy, Beijing
  • ForTeachers and tech coaches
Conference details →See the ISB case
How the hour runs

A case, the reasons it worked, and a live build

  • The case: what students built in five days

    Over five days, ISB students built working websites and apps with AI and presented them to judges from Harvard, Stanford, Y Combinator, and AI labs.

  • Why it worked

    The program starts from lasting skills like creativity and judgment, with AI strengthening the learning instead of replacing it.

  • Build a website, live, with the room

    We start from a pre-built site and add the sections and features you call out, so you see what it takes to turn ideas into a working website.

  • Take it back to your school

    You leave with a planning checklist and a guide to accessing AI tools on school networks.
