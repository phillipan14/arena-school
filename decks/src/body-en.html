The question for every family in this room

When AI can draft an essay in seconds, what must our children still learn to do?

The answer follows from what is already happening at work. So we start there.

Nobody can predict exact jobs in 2040. But we can read what is already happening, and the direction is clear.

Part 1 · What we are seeing

Work is being rewritten, in China and worldwide, faster than most families realize.

First the evidence. Everything we recommend follows from it.

Two numbers on the scale of the change, then four careers parents have always trusted.

Doctor · Lawyer · Banker · Engineer

Even the safest careers are being rewritten.

The four paths families have trusted for generations. One number from each.

For decades these were the safe answers. Each of the next four slides shows how much of that job AI already does, from a named hospital, court, bank, and company.

The safe middle is gone

Being average is no longer safe. Companies keep the people who work well with AI.

Accenture now exits staff it cannot retrain for AI. At Microsoft, using AI is “no longer optional.” At Duolingo, it counts in performance reviews.

Julie Sweet, Accenture earnings call, Sept 25, 2025; Julia Liuson, Microsoft, internal email, June 2025; Luis von Ahn, Duolingo memo, April 28, 2025

Accenture's CEO: "We are exiting on a compressed timeline, people where reskilling ... is not a viable path." Its workforce shrank by more than 11,000 in one quarter while it doubled its AI and data staff to 77,000 in two years. The message from employers is the same everywhere: the skill protects you.

The losses sit where AI replaces tasks. Where AI complements people, employment is flat or rising. Entry-level work is exactly where our children will start.

Part 2 · Therefore, the essential skills

Therefore, three skills now decide who gets ahead: agency, taste, and craft.

Children who build them will lead. Children who don’t will start behind. Employers are already asking for them.

Cornell University's 2026 report put it plainly: "People must be good at deciding what to do and why." Here is what employers now ask for.

What the evidence points to

The skills that set children apart: agency, taste, and craft.

  1. 01Agency

    Choosing what’s worth solving

    Deciding what to make before anyone asks

  2. 02Taste

    Telling which version actually works

    Checking AI’s work before anyone relies on it

  3. 03Craft

    Finishing it well

    Turning a first draft into something people trust

Agency is what the employers who reward initiative are paying for. Taste is the 50% who put checking AI's output first. Craft is finishing: the part AI's first draft never does.

Build now, point 3 · make AI a tutor

The same AI can be a shortcut or a patient tutor. Teach the tutor.

Shortcut

“Just give me the answer.”

The homework gets done. The thinking gets skipped.

Tutor

“Explain it until I get it.”

The child does the thinking. AI explains and checks.

OECD, PISA 2025 results, Sept 8, 2026: moderate, intentional AI use went with stronger learning, though the study does not prove one causes the other

Same tool, opposite result. The difference is the habit, and habits are built at home.

Build now, point 4 · three habits for home

Start three habits at home this week.

  1. 1
    Agency

    Make one real thing together

    A chore chart, a trip plan, or a quiz for a younger sibling

  2. 2
    Taste

    Ask, “How did you check it?”

    Treat every AI answer as a first draft

  3. 3
    Craft

    Show it to a real person

    A grandparent, a teacher, or a friend

Each habit trains one of the three skills. None needs special software, and each takes minutes.

And yet

This is one of the best times ever to be a child, and the best time to build.

When we were young, almost everything was done by hand. Today a child with an idea and AI can build something real, and the ones who start now pull ahead.

Everything so far is the warning. This is the opportunity: the same tools that rewrite jobs put real building within a child's reach.

Hold onto this

Any AI tool takes an afternoon to learn. The three skills take years, so start now.

Tools change every few months. Choosing a problem, judging the result, and finishing the work carry into every job, and the lead compounds.

A child who starts at twelve has years of practice by the first job. That head start is the comparative advantage.

If you remember one thing

When AI can make almost anything, students need to know what’s worth making, whether it’s good, and how to finish it.

The children who start building now will pull ahead. There has never been a better time to begin.
