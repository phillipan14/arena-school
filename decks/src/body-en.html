Those asks add up to three skills: agency, taste, and craft.

Agency

Choosing what’s worth solving

At workThe person who defines the problem directs the work.

Taste

Telling which version actually works

At workAI offers many versions; someone has to pick one and say why.

Craft

Three small habits at home help children use AI with intention.

Make one small thing together

A chore list, a trip plan, or a quiz for a younger sibling

Ask, “How did you check it?”

Treat AI’s answer as a first draft

Show it to a real person

A grandparent, a teacher, or a friend
