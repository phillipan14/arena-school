A question to start先问大家一个问题

Think back to your first job. What did you learn that first year?回想一下您的第一份工作。
那一年，您学到了什么？

For most people, the first job is where judgment is learned: small, routine tasks, done again and again, with someone checking the work.对很多人来说，判断力是在第一份工作里练出来的：琐碎的日常活儿一遍遍地做，旁边有人帮你把关。

More and more of that routine work is now done with AI’s help. So where will young people learn judgment?现在，这类日常工作越来越多是借助 AI 完成的。那年轻人的判断力，要去哪里练？

What’s changing at work职场在发生什么变化

AI is changing jobs more than removing them.AI 更多是在改变工作，而不是让工作消失。

1 in 4

workers worldwide are in jobs exposed to generative AI. The most likely effect is that the job changes.全球约四分之一劳动者的工作会受到生成式 AI 的影响。最可能的结果，是工作本身发生变化。

ILO and NASK, May 2025
39%

of workers’ core skills are expected to change by 2030, down from 44% two years earlier.劳动者核心技能预计到 2030 年发生变化的比例，低于两年前预测的 44%。

World Economic Forum, Future of Jobs 2025
170M vs 92M

jobs expected to be created vs displaced by 2030, a net gain of 78 million.到 2030 年预计新创造和被取代的岗位数量，相抵后净增 7800 万个。

World Economic Forum, Future of Jobs 2025

The question for children today is less “will there be work?” and more “what will the work ask of them?”对孩子们来说，与其问“以后还有没有工作”，不如问“将来的工作需要他们具备什么”。

Entry-level work初级岗位

The first job now asks for judgment earlier.第一份工作，就开始考验判断力。

7×

Entry-level roles that use AI are seven times more likely to ask for skills once expected of senior people, like judgment and leadership.用到 AI 的初级岗位，被要求具备判断力、领导力等过去只对资深员工提的能力，概率是其他岗位的七倍。

PwC Global AI Jobs Barometer, June 2026
+35%

growth in those AI-exposed entry-level roles since 2019, while other entry-level roles fell 10%.是这类受 AI 影响的初级岗位 2019 年以来的增幅；同期其他初级岗位反而减少了 10%。

PwC Global AI Jobs Barometer, June 2026

Young people who use AI well, and can judge what it gives them, are trusted with bigger decisions sooner.会用 AI、也能判断它给的东西好不好的年轻人，会更早被信任去做更大的决定。

At work today今天的职场

Candidates now show what they can do.求职者现在用作品说话。

Day to day at an AI companyAI 公司的日常
  • Everyone prototypes, not only engineers. A marketer mocks up a tool before asking for one.不只是工程师，人人都在做原型。做市场的同事会先自己搭个工具雏形，再去提需求。
  • The first draft is cheap. The discussion is about which version is right.出一版初稿几乎没有成本，大家讨论的是哪个版本才对。
  • People are judged on what they decided, and why.看一个人行不行，看的是他做了什么决定、为什么这么定。
An interview, recently最近的一次面试

A candidate for a growth role at an AI company built a small tool for the team’s social media work before the interview, showed it live, and got the offer. Nobody asked for it. That was the point.有位候选人应聘一家 AI 公司的增长岗位，面试前先给团队的社交媒体工作做了个小工具，当场演示，拿到了 offer。没人要求这么做，关键恰恰就在这里。

Walking into an interview prepared.有备而来，走进面试。

A candidate has a few days before an interview. Turning one résumé and one job posting into likely questions, rehearsed answers, and a study plan takes hours, and twice as long if they practice in two languages.离面试只剩几天。要根据一份简历和一份职位描述，整理出可能被问的问题、练熟回答、排好复习计划，得花好几个小时；要是中英文都得练，时间还要翻倍。

What it actually took其实靠的是什么

The skill is not coding. It is knowing what you want.关键不是写代码，而是清楚自己想要什么。

1Know, generally, what outcome you want大致知道你想要什么结果
2If needed, brainstorm with your coding agent first需要的话，先和AI 编程助手一起头脑风暴
3Ask it directly what it needs from you直接问它需要你提供什么
4Once there’s a first output, feed it feedback like crazy第一版出来后，使劲提意见

You don’t need to know how it’s built. You need to know what it is when it’s done.不用知道它是怎么做出来的，只要知道做成之后它应该是什么样。

Talk through the best way to implement it and the caveats.聊聊怎么实现最好，有哪些要注意的地方。

“What information do you need from me to begin the build?”“开始做之前，你需要我提供哪些信息？”

Treat yourself as a product manager giving feedback to a team member who takes instructions very literally.把自己当成产品经理，给一个只会照字面执行指令的队友提反馈。

These are habits anyone can practice, at any age.这些习惯，不管多大年纪都能练。

Why these three为什么是这三项

Agency, taste, and craft.主动性、审美与匠心。

When AI can make almost anything, what matters is knowing what’s worth making, whether it’s good, and how to finish it.AI 几乎什么都能做出来的时候，要紧的是知道什么值得做、做得好不好，以及怎么把它做完。

Agency主动性

Choosing what’s worth solving.选出真正值得解决的问题。

Every useful tool starts with deciding a task is worth fixing.每个有用的工具，都是从认定“这件事值得改”开始的。

At workTasks that only follow instructions are the easiest to hand to AI; people who define the problem direct the work.在工作中只是照指令办事的工作，最容易交给 AI；能定义问题的人，才是主导工作的人。

Taste审美

Telling which version actually works.分辨哪个版本真正行得通。

AI offers many versions; the work is asking which one people will actually use.AI 能给出很多版本；真正的功夫，是判断大家到底会用哪一个。

At workTeams can generate ten versions in an hour; the person who picks the right one, and can say why, is the one they rely on.在工作中团队一小时就能做出十个版本；能挑出对的那个、还说得清为什么的人，才是大家信得过的人。

Craft匠心

Finishing it well.把事情做完，并且做好。

The first draft comes fast. Making something people can rely on takes care.初稿来得很快；要做成别人用得放心的东西，得花心思。

At workWorking products earn trust in a way prototypes and slide decks do not.在工作中真正能跑起来的产品，比原型和 PPT 更能赢得信任。

“People must be good at deciding what to do and why.”“人必须善于判断该做什么、为什么做。”
Cornell University, Report of the Committee on the Future of the American University, September 2026
From teaching来自教学一线

Three lessons from the classroom.课堂上的三点体会。

01
Agency主动性

Choosing is the hard part.难的是选题。

Students pick up the tools in an afternoon. Choosing a problem worth solving takes longer, and matters more.工具，学生一个下午就能上手；找到一个值得解决的问题，要花更久，也更要紧。

02
Taste审美

Real feedback makes it better.真实反馈，让作品更好。

Live feedback from real users (a classmate, a colleague, or an instructor) is how students improve faster, and how a working product becomes a better one.真实用户（同学、同事或老师）当场给的反馈，让学生进步更快，也让一个能用的产品变得更好用。

03
Craft匠心

Finishing is where they grow.把事做完，人才会成长。

The first version comes fast. Finishing it so others can rely on it is where students grow the most.第一版来得很快；把它打磨到别人能放心用，学生在这一步成长最多。

At home在家里

The question is not whether, but how.问题不是要不要用，而是怎么用。

72.6% of parents in China support their child using AI, most of them with conditions.72.6% 的中国家长支持孩子用 AI，但大多附带条件。

China Youth Daily poll, May 2026

The OECD’s newest data suggests moderate, intentional use may go with stronger learning.OECD 最新数据显示，适度、有目的地使用 AI，可能和更好的学习表现有关。

OECD PISA 2025, September 2026
Small things families can do alongside school除了学校，家里也可以做的几件小事

Make one small thing together.和孩子一起做个小东西。

A family chore list, a trip plan, or a quiz for a younger sibling.一张家务清单、一份旅行计划，或者给弟弟妹妹出的一套小测验。

Ask, “How did you check it?”问一句：“你是怎么核对的？”

Treat AI’s answer as a first draft, and talk about what was wrong with it.把 AI 的回答当作初稿，聊聊它哪里不对。

Show it to a real person.拿给真人看看。

A grandparent, a teacher, or a friend. Real feedback is where taste comes from.爷爷奶奶、老师、朋友都行。审美，就是从真实的反馈里来的。
