Our philosophy

When AI can make almost anything, students need to know what's worth making, whether it's good, and how to finish it.

Agency

Choosing what's worth solving.

AI can help make almost anything. Deciding what is worth making, and why, is where students' own thinking matters most.

Taste

Telling which version works.

AI helps students try many versions quickly. They learn to judge which one works for the people using it.

Craft

Finishing it well.

A first draft comes sooner. Turning it into something people can rely on is the student's work, and it takes care.

Design thinking, with AI

The design cycle is the right frame for AI.

AI speeds up the middle of the cycle. The parts that make a designer (framing the problem, judging options, evaluating honestly) matter more, not less.

A
AgencyInquiring & analysing

TasteDeveloping ideas

AI makes it cheap to prototype many options. The skill is choosing and justifying one.

How we teach with AI

AI as a partner students direct, never a shortcut.

Prompt & critique

Students write the prompts, question the output, and check it for mistakes.

Just in time

Each method is taught the hour before it's used: interviews before user research, pricing the day it goes live.

Real audiences

Work is shown to users and practitioners every day, not only to the teacher.

Honest process

Students credit AI and can explain every decision, in line with the IB's approach of acknowledging AI use.

The same stance guides our teacher work: AI supports your planning and materials, and your judgment stays in charge.
