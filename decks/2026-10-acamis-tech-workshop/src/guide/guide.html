Usually not available without extra setup

ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini (personal accounts), Perplexity, Midjourney, and Cursor do not officially serve mainland China. Websites published on vercel.app addresses are often blocked as well, so publish student work to an address your school can open.

If your school uses Microsoft 365

Microsoft 365 Copilot is not available on the China version of Microsoft 365 (operated by 21Vianet). Schools on a global Microsoft 365 setup should check their own license with IT.

Before any class

  • Test each tool on the student network, not only the staff network.
  • Keep a backup: a phone hotspot, or a second tool that does the same job.
  • Use placeholder data. No real student names or records in any tool.
  • Agree on accounts and logins with your IT team before students sign up.

Students and age rules

  • Most of these services say they are mainly for adults and require guardian consent for users under 18; several add extra steps for under-14s.
  • China's Personal Information Protection Law treats under-14s' personal information as sensitive and requires guardian consent (Articles 28 and 31).
  • Vidu is 18+ only and is left off this list. Claude requires users to be 18+; ChatGPT requires 13+ with parent permission under 18.
