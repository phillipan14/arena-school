The question every parent here is asking

When AI can draft an essay in seconds, what should children still learn to do?

Nobody knows exactly what work will look like when they graduate. So today sticks to what is already happening at work, and what you can try at home this week.

What intentional use looks like

The same AI can be a shortcut or a patient tutor.

Shortcut

“Just give me the answer.”

The homework gets done. The thinking gets skipped.

Tutor

“Explain it until I get it.”

The child does the thinking. AI explains and checks.

In PISA 2025, moderate, intentional AI use went with stronger learning, though the study does not prove one causes the other.1

1OECD, PISA 2025 results, Sept 8, 2026

Agency, taste, and craft at home

Three small habits to try at home this week.

  1. 1
    Agency

    Make one small thing together

    A chore list, a trip plan, or a quiz for a younger sibling

  2. 2
    Taste

    Ask, “How did you check it?”

    Treat AI’s answer as a first draft

  3. 3
    Craft

    Show it to a real person

    A grandparent, a teacher, or a friend

Hold onto this

A new AI tool takes an afternoon to learn. The three skills take far longer.

Children will switch AI tools many times. Choosing a problem, judging the result, and finishing the work carry into every job.

If you remember one thing

When AI can make almost anything, students need to know what’s worth making, whether it’s good, and how to finish it.
