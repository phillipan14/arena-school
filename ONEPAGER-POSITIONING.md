# One-pager positioning (MESSAGING-BRIEF, Oct 17, 2026)

What each one-pager says, to whom, and why it is true. Every claim below is on the live site or in
STATS-SOURCES.md / PAST-TALKS-SOURCES.md; no outcome is promised. Built by `tools/one-pager/build.py`.

## Parent
- **Motivation:** college applications; real AI skills; worry that school is not preparing their child for AI-era
  work; skills that lead to internships and jobs.
- **What we offer:** your child builds an app with AI tools, launches it at a live link, gets real users, and pitches
  it to a panel (workshops and five-day intensives at school; year-long 1:1 mentorship families enroll in directly).
- **Proof:** International School of Beijing, summer 2026: students built working apps with AI and presented to judges
  from Harvard, Stanford, Y Combinator, and AI labs (event and results pages). Bodley Academy, Hong Kong, 2025: a
  student launched a sign-language-to-speech app in five days (results page). Mentorship milestone ladder: launch,
  traction, revenue, impact, recognition (mentorship page). Why now: over 80% of U.S. students say teachers did not
  explicitly teach them to use AI for schoolwork; 54% already use AI for school (RAND RR-A4180-1, STATS-SOURCES.md).
- **Why we are qualified:** mentors from a team that studied at Harvard, Caltech, Brown, and Tsinghua and worked at
  McKinsey, Goldman Sachs, Y Combinator, Apple, and Microsoft AI; a member of the Anthropic Startup Program and the
  OpenAI Partner Network. What the child keeps: a live product, a documented record for college, summer program,
  scholarship, and internship applications (portfolio workshop, mentorship pages). Admissions rest with each university.

## Teacher (and school leaders)
- **Motivation:** not falling behind; confident staff; safe, compliant use with student data; time back; tools that
  work in a real class tomorrow.
- **What we offer:** hands-on training where each teacher brings one task from a class they teach now and leaves with a
  working tool on the school's approved systems (live session, department workshop, or school-wide); a five-level
  fluency baseline, one level up a term; student data stays in school-approved accounts.
- **Proof:** staff training at Schwarzman College, Tsinghua University (results page); ACAMIS Technology Conference
  workshop, Oct 18, 2026 (event page); tools teachers made, e.g. a poem annotator and a feedback helper by Ben, an
  English teacher at Shanghai American School (teacher page). Why now: 53% of ELA, math, and science teachers used AI
  for school in 2025; about 1 in 2 districts trained teachers by fall 2024; weekly users report about 5.9 hours saved a
  week (RAND, Gallup; STATS-SOURCES.md).
- **Why we are qualified:** the same team and memberships as above; we work inside the school's approved platforms and
  its data-protection policy.

## General
- **Who:** Arena School, hands-on AI programs. **What:** people build real things with AI and judge them with real
  users. **For whom:** students, educators and schools, families, university and early career; each tile states that
  audience's own reason. **Why us:** run at the International School of Beijing, Bodley Academy, and Schwarzman College;
  the team and memberships above.
- **Plain meaning of agency, taste, craft (from the approach page):** choose a real problem and pitch it; test with real
  users and judge which version works; finish, launch at a live link, and explain every decision.
