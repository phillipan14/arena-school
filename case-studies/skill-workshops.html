Case studies · Skill workshops

Portfolio workshop: everything you’ve built, in one website.

A one- or two-day workshop where students build their own portfolio website: every project, activity and award in one place, designed with their own taste, live at their own web address.

At a glance
FormatHands-on workshop, laptops open all day
LengthOne or two days
Who it’s forGrades 9 to 12, especially juniors and seniors preparing applications
Group sizeUp to about 20 students
OutcomeA live portfolio website at the student’s own address
BringA laptop, plus photos, write-ups and links for everything they have done
LanguageEnglish or Chinese
Why a website

An application form flattens years of work into lists.

On an application, a robotics team, a research project and a design portfolio all become a line of text with a character limit. A portfolio website lets a reader actually see the work: the robot moving, the app running, the design the way it was meant to look.

It won’t replace the application. It makes everything in it easier to read and harder to forget, and it shows, before anyone asks, that the student can build.

What students build

Three things that make a portfolio worth opening.

01

A site that looks like them

A distinctive design shaped by their own taste and profile, not a template anyone could have picked.

02

Everything, organized

Extracurriculars, projects, research, competitions and awards, arranged so a reader understands the whole story in a few minutes.

03

Work people can interact with

If a student built a robot, we help them vibe code a simulation visitors can play with. If they ran research, an interactive chart. Proof you can click.

How it runs

One day builds the site. Two days add the interactive pieces.

Morning, day one

Inventory and story: what to show, in what order, and what it says about the student.

Afternoon, day one

Design and build with an AI coding agent. The site goes live by the end of the day.

Day two

Interactive pieces such as simulations and demos, a design pass, and a final publish.
