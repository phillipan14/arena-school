How we work · Portfolio workshop

Portfolio workshop: everything you’ve built, in one website.

In one or two days, each student builds a live portfolio site at their own address, designed with their own taste, that makes their work easy to see.

New this school year · grades 9 to 12 · up to about 20 students · one or two days · English or Chinese

Why a website

An application form turns years of work into lines of text.

A robotics season, a research project and a design portfolio each become one line with a character limit. A portfolio site lets a reader see the work itself: the robot moving, the app running, the design as it was meant to look. It doesn’t replace the application. It makes the application easier to read and shows that the student can make things.

What students build

Three things that make a portfolio worth opening.

01 · Taste

A site that looks like them

Students compare design directions and choose the one that fits their story, so the site feels like theirs, not a template.

02

Everything, organized

Projects, research, competitions and awards, arranged so a reader understands the whole story in a few minutes.

03 · Craft

Work people can try

A robot becomes a simulation visitors can play with; research becomes an interactive chart. Students build it with AI tools and finish it with care.

How it runs

Day one, students build the site. Day two, they add the interactive pieces.

  1. Day 1 · morning
    Inventory and story

    What to show, in what order, and what it says about the student.

  2. Day 1 · afternoon
    Design, build, go live

    Students design and build the site with AI tools, and it goes live by the end of the day.

  3. Day 2
    Interactive pieces, final publish

    Simulations and demos, a design pass, and a final publish.

Offer it after a student intensive, or on its own for juniors and seniors preparing applications.
