Case studies · Parent talks

Parent talks: the future of education, and what parents can do now.

A one- to two-hour seminar for parent communities. AI is moving faster than any school can. This talk is about how families can adapt now, instead of waiting.

At a glance
FormatTalk, live demo and open Q&A
Length60 to 120 minutes
Who it’s forParent communities at schools, from middle school to grade 12, and parents of older students
Group sizeAny room
LanguageEnglish or Chinese, with bilingual slides
OutcomeA clear first step for every age group, and something useful for parents themselves
What the talk covers

Honest about the change. Practical about what to do.

01

Why schools can’t move at AI speed

Institutions change over years. The technology changes over months. That isn’t a criticism of schools, it’s how institutions work, and it’s why families can’t simply wait.

02

What’s changing in college and work

What admissions readers and employers are starting to look for, with no hype and no statistics we can’t stand behind.

03

What parents can do now

A first step for each age, from middle school play to a senior’s portfolio, so students stay competitive in school now and at work later.

04

A live demo

An idea from someone in the room, built into a working app in minutes. The part parents talk about at dinner.

Where it has run

Delivered for international school parent communities in Beijing.
