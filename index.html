What we teach

The rules for school, jobs, and careers were written before AI. We teach what still sets people apart.

When AI can make almost anything, what matters is knowing what’s worth making, whether it’s good, and how to finish it. We build all three early, alongside teachers and families.

I · Agency

AI can help make almost anything, so the real work is deciding what’s worth making. Students choose their own project on day one; mentors support, students decide.

How we teach it →

In practice

  1. Pitch their own idea in three minutes, no slides.
  2. Ask an expert for fifteen minutes of advice.
  3. Set a personal goal to keep for ninety days.

II · Taste

AI helps students try many versions. Showing their work to users and mentors every day teaches them which one actually works, and why.

How we teach it →

In practice

  1. Put a prototype online at a public link by midway.
  2. Work through the product with an experienced mentor.
  3. Test it with five questions, then defend each answer.

III · Craft

AI gets students to a first draft sooner. Turning it into something reliable is their own work, and every build ends in a live demo.

How we teach it →

In practice

  1. Build with AI coding tools, making every call themselves.
  2. Stop at a fixed freeze and demo what works.
  3. Write a one-page personal routine to take home.
