What we teach

Much of how we prepare young people for work took shape before AI. We teach what still sets people apart.

When AI can make almost anything, what matters is knowing what’s worth making, whether it’s good, and how to finish it. We build all three early, alongside teachers and families.

I · Agency

AI can help make almost anything, so the real work is deciding what’s worth making. Students choose their own project on day one; mentors support, students decide.

How we teach it →

What it changes

  1. In schoolWhen AI can help finish an assignment, the harder skill is setting the question, not answering it.
  2. At workTasks that only follow instructions are the easiest to hand to AI; people who define the problem direct the work.
  3. Over a careerA project a student chose and saw through shows initiative in a way a transcript cannot.

II · Taste

AI helps students try many versions. Showing their work to users and mentors every day teaches them which one actually works, and why.

How we teach it →

What it changes

  1. In schoolWhen every essay and project can look polished, telling strong work from weak becomes what teachers need to see.
  2. At workTeams can generate ten versions in an hour; the person who picks the right one, and can say why, is the one they rely on.
  3. Over a careerJudgment compounds: it is what earns trust with larger decisions over time.

III · Craft

AI gets students to a first draft sooner. Turning it into something reliable is their own work, and every build ends in a live demo.

How we teach it →

What it changes

  1. In schoolA first draft is now instant; revising it into something reliable is where the learning happens.
  2. At workWorking products earn trust in a way prototypes and slide decks do not.
  3. Over a careerA record of finished work, with a live link, speaks for itself in applications and interviews.
