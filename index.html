What we teach
01 / 03Agency

Choosing what’s worth solving.

AI can help make almost anything. Deciding what is worth making, and why, is where students’ own thinking matters most.

02 / 03Taste

Telling which version actually works.

AI helps students try many versions quickly. They learn to see which one works for the people using it.

03 / 03Craft

Finishing it well.

AI helps students reach a first draft sooner. Turning it into something people can rely on is their work.
