02

Staff AI fluency

Five levels; every teacher moves up one a term
  1. 1UnawareHasn’t used AI, or avoids it.
  2. 2CuriousTries chat tools now and then.
  3. 3PractitionerUses approved tools weekly for planning, feedback, and admin.
  4. 4DesignerBuilds AI into lessons, and teaches students to use it responsibly.
  5. 5BuilderCreates tools for colleagues, and leads others.
  • BaselineA short self-assessment places every teacher.
  • One level upEach group trains up one level.
  • Each termRe-measure, so leaders see the curve shift.
  • CoachesLevel 5 teachers coach colleagues.
03

How a teacher moves up one level

01Before
Each teacher brings one task from a class they teach now.
02Show
We make one tool live from a plain-language description.
03Make
Each teacher makes theirs on your approved tools and checks every result.
04Keep
Teachers keep the tool and the prompt, to reuse and share.

05

Student data guardrails

Whose account is it?
Personal account
  • Consumer terms
  • No data agreement
  • Not for student data
School-approved platform
  • Enterprise agreement
  • Agreed by the school
  • Approved for student data
  • Inside your approved platformsWe work only in the accounts and tools your school approves, and agree the data rules with your team before any session.
  • No student data in personal accountsStudent names, work, and records never go into a personal or consumer account.
  • Sample data for practiceTeacher sessions use teachers’ own materials or anonymized sample data, not student records.
