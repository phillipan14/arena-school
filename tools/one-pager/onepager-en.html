Learning approach

Learners decide. AI builds. Real people judge.

One framework runs every program. The learner sits at the center; four strands of work circle them: inquiry and planning, research and evaluation, creating and communicating, and collaboration and reflection.

AI fluency for the whole staff

A few early adopters are there. We bring everyone else up.

  1. 1Unaware

A baseline places every teacher on five levels; we train each group up one level and re-measure each term.

Student data comes first

Before which tool, ask: whose account is it?

  • Inside your approved platforms. We work only in the accounts and tools your school approves, and agree the data rules with your team before any session.
  • No student data in personal accounts. Student names, work, and records never go into a personal or consumer account.
  • Sample data for practice. Teacher sessions use teachers’ own materials or anonymized sample data, not student records.
  • AI use disclosed. Learners say which steps they handed to AI, and how they used it.
