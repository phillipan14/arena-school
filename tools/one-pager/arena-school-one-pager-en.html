How we teach

Learners decide. AI builds. Real people judge.

  • AgencyChoose what is worth doing.
  • TasteJudge whether it is actually good.
  • CraftFinish it so people can trust it.
Four strands, assessed on every project
  • Inquiry and planningTurn an interest into a focused question, and plan the work.
  • Research and evaluationCheck sources, including AI output, and choose on evidence.
  • Creating and communicatingBuild and test it with AI, and explain it clearly.
  • Collaboration and reflectionUse critique from teachers and peers to revise the work.
03

Staff AI fluency

Five levels; every teacher moves up one a term
  1. 1UnawareHasn’t used AI, or avoids it.
  2. 2CuriousTries chat tools now and then.
  3. 3PractitionerUses approved tools weekly for planning, feedback, and admin.
  4. 4DesignerBuilds AI into lessons, and teaches students to use it responsibly.
  5. 5BuilderCreates tools for colleagues, and leads others.
  • BaselineA short self-assessment places every teacher.
  • One level upEach group trains up one level.
  • Each termRe-measure, so leaders see the curve shift.
  • CoachesLevel 5 teachers coach colleagues.
