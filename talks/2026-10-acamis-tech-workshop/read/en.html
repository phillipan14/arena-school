The research · two 2025 studies

Used as a shortcut, AI does the thinking. Set up as a coach, it helps students learn more.

High school math · nearly 1,000 students
−17%

exam grades after practicing with plain ChatGPT. A tutor version that gave hints, not answers, largely avoided the drop.

Harvard physics · a randomized trial
2×

the learning gains of an active-learning class, in less time, with an AI tutor built on the same methods

Bastani and others, PNAS, June 25, 2025: grades fell 17% once plain ChatGPT was taken away; with safeguards, the harm was “largely mitigated”; Kestin and others, Scientific Reports, June 3, 2025: 194 Harvard physics students, median learning gains more than double

Name the scarce skills

Agency chooses, taste judges, and craft finishes.

The three skills
  1. 01AgencyDecide what is worth building before anyone assigns it.
  2. 02TasteJudge whether the output is actually good.
  3. 03CraftFinish it so people can trust it.

How the week was designed

Each skill had a moment in the week that students couldn’t skip.

Skill · how the week trains it
  1. 01AgencyStudents pitch their own idea in three minutes, with no slides, and choose the problem on day one.
  2. 02TasteA prototype is online by midweek, so real users and mentors react to it every day.
  3. 03CraftBuilding stops at a fixed hour, and students explain every decision live on demo day.

Is using AI just cheating?

No. AI tools write the code, and students make every decision.

AI tools and mentors handle
  • Writing the code
  • Putting it online
  • Fixing technical errors
Students decide
  • What to build, and for whom
  • Which version is better, and why
  • When it’s good enough to ship

What students need from us

Skills grow from practice with structure, so your role becomes the coach.

  1. A real projectwith something at stake
  2. A coachwho asks how you checked it
  3. Honest feedbackfrom real people

Try this on Monday · three coaching questions

Three questions turn any AI task into practice in agency, taste, and craft.

  1. “Why this problem?”Agency: they choose, not the AI
  2. “How do you know it’s good?”Taste: they judge what it made
  3. “What would make someone trust it?”Craft: they check and finish it
