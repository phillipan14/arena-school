Explore AI through a project you care about

Your starting point can be history, language, art, the environment, or a community need. AI can help you make an interactive version of an idea while you learn how it works.

Four useful categories

Chat and research assistants help you discuss and investigate. Coding agents work with project files and tests. General task agents carry out work across tools. Specialised creation tools focus on outputs such as websites, games, and designs. These categories overlap; choose the product mode for your task.

Choose the tool and the right mode

Use tools your teacher permits. School access to Canva does not automatically mean every AI feature is enabled. Product access, limits, and account requirements vary.

A food-waste tracker using Canva

  1. Create a short page explaining what the diary is for.
  2. Use a form connected to Sheets to collect a few simple entries.
  3. Enter fictional data first. Reload and check that it remains saved.
  4. Review the records and decide what a useful summary would show.
  5. Try the process with someone and revise what is confusing.

This is a proposed project workflow. A tracker that people fill in is different from a device that automatically weighs food; the latter also needs hardware and integration.

Other projects you might explore

Humanities: an interactive oral-history exhibition with consented recordings and source notes. Arts: a digital exhibition where visitors explore the story behind each work. Education: a language game or an interactive lesson. Community: a mobile-friendly guide to verified local services.

Give the tool enough context

We want to help families understand what food they throw away. Help us design a one-week diary that takes two minutes a day. Ask what you need to know first. Explain how entries will be saved, and use fictional examples while we test it.

Use feedback to improve the result

This diary asks for too much detail. Make a version with three simple entries per day. Explain what information we would lose, so we can decide whether that tradeoff is acceptable.

Keep your own reasoning visible

Record why you chose the problem, how AI helped, what you checked, and what you changed. Read original sources, test real tasks, and do not present generated interviews as evidence. Protect other people’s information and follow your teacher’s AI-use rules.

Can this become a WeChat mini program?

Possibly as a further development project, but it is not the same as publishing a website. It needs a separate platform workflow and review. Start by testing the idea in a mobile-friendly web prototype. Tencent’s publishing guide explains one supported route.
