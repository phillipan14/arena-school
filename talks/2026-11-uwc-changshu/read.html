08. Choose the product that matches the work you want it to do.

Read slide text
ProductUseful starting taskImportant distinction
DeepSeek chatExplain a concept, discuss an approach, or draft code.Code in a reply is not the same as a tested, published app.
DeepSeek HarnessWork on files and run commands or tests in a project.A separate agent product in preview; requires setup.
Kimi Build / 3D MakeDevelop a website or explore a 3D model.Choose the dedicated mode; inspect the actual output.

A tool’s ability to accept images is different from its ability to write and execute code.

11. A food-waste project could involve research, a diary, and a conversation.

Project taskA possible Canva-only routeHow your team checks it
Explain the purposePublish a short website explaining the diary.Can a family understand what to record?
Collect entriesUse a form connected to Canva Sheets; use sample entries first.Does each submission persist after reloading? Who can see it?
Explore the recordsReview the Sheet and create a summary for discussion.Do totals match the entries? What can the data actually tell you?

13. What would convince you that the idea actually works?

Read slide text
Claim to test

“Families can complete
our diary in two minutes.”

Observe a real attempt

Give someone the diary. Watch without explaining each step.

Compare the result with your claim

Time it. Notice missing entries and confusing questions.

Make a decision

Keep what works. Simplify what does not, then try again.

14. Explain what is wrong and ask for a specific revision.

Read slide text
First draft

Too much to enter

Weight of each item

Price paid

Food category

Reason for disposal

Storage conditions

Your feedback
“This takes too long. Keep three simple entries per day. Explain what information we lose so we can decide whether that tradeoff is acceptable.”

A useful revision names the problem and the constraint. “Make it better” leaves both unclear.
