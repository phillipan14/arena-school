Why it matters now

When AI can make almost anything, what a student chooses, judges, and finishes sets them apart.

Over one sustained project, students learn how a real product is made, how to use the new tools well, and how to keep making their own, for passions, hobbies, and schoolwork. It also gives a college application real, verifiable evidence of initiative. Admissions decisions rest with each university; we do not promise an outcome.

  • Their project, their decisions

    A tutor assigns tasks. Here, the student chooses the problem and answers for the results, with a mentor beside them, not in front.

How it works

Four phases, one project, at the student’s own pace.

01 / 04

Every week, a mentor holds a 25-minute check-in. Between sessions, the student sends a short written update, and families review progress each term.

Milestones

Five milestones, the same ladder for every project.

Each student sets a target with their mentor at the start, then moves up the ladder at their own pace, judged on the work, not on attendance.

  1. 01Launch

    A working product, published at a public link. Every project starts here.

  2. 02Traction

    100 users from outside the student’s own circle.

  3. 03Revenue

    First income from people who pay for the product.

  4. 04Impact

    1,000 users, press coverage, or a competition final.

  5. 05Recognition

    A national competition win, or a registered company.

Questions

What families ask.

What if my child doesn’t have an idea yet?+

That is where most students start. In the first phase, the student and mentor explore problems the student cares about and test which one is worth building.

Does this fit a busy schedule?+

Yes. The weekly check-in is 25 minutes, and a written update between sessions takes a few minutes. The program is designed to sit alongside exams, sports, and other commitments.

Does my child need a team, or can they work alone?+

Either works. Teams of up to four are encouraged, and students may also work on their own. Students can regroup at the end of a term.

Will this help with college admissions?+

No program can promise an admissions outcome, and we do not. What a student gains is a real project with users, results, and decisions they can describe in their own words and show with a link. Each university makes its own decision.

What does it cost?+

It depends on the project and the length of mentorship. Tell us about your child and their project idea, and we will confirm details directly within three working days.
