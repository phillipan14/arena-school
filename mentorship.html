The details

Logistics and questions.

My child has a busy schedule already. Does this fit?+

The weekly check-in is 25 minutes, and the written updates take a few minutes. It's built to sit alongside exams, sports and everything else.

Does my child need a team, or can they go solo?+

Either works. Teams of up to four are encouraged, and a solo founder runs a real company too. Students can regroup at the end of a term.

What if we miss a session or two?+

Milestones are judged by proof, not attendance. A missed week doesn't reset the ladder; the next check-in just covers more.

Does this count for IB CAS hours?+

A service-oriented project can qualify. Talk to your IB coordinator, and we're glad to help document the work.
