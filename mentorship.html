Year-Long Project Mentorship.

Students develop one project from October to March, with a mentor check-in every week.

  • WhoGrades 7 to 12
  • LengthOctober to March
  • Check-insWeekly, 25 min, in person or online
  • Teams1 to 4 students
  • KickoffFirst week of October

What students produce

What students finish with.

One project, developed over six months, by a student or a team.

The productA live product

Students build it with AI tools and make the decisions: the design, user interviews, and pricing.

The recordDocumented work

Six months of users, numbers, and decisions that students can describe in applications and interviews.

PresentingCompetitions

Mentors help students choose suitable competitions to enter as the project grows.

How it works

Students lead the work. A mentor meets each team weekly.

  1. Every weekCheck-in

    A mentor reviews progress with the team, helps with problems, and agrees the next goal.

  2. Between check-insWritten update

    A few lines: what was finished, what was learned, and what comes next.

  3. End of each termFamily review

    Families see the work, and students can regroup for the next term.

Milestones

Five milestones, the same for every project.

Each project moves at its own pace. Progress is judged on the work, not on attendance.

  1. 01Launch

    A working product, published at a public link. Every project starts here.

  2. 02Traction

    100 users from outside the student’s own circle.

  3. 03Revenue

    First income from people who pay for the product.

  4. 04Impact

    1,000 users, press coverage, or a competition final.

  5. 05Recognition

    A national competition win, or a registered company.

Questions

What families ask.

My child has a busy schedule already. Does this fit?+

The weekly check-in is 25 minutes, and the written updates take a few minutes. The program is designed to sit alongside exams, sports, and other commitments.

Does my child need a team, or can they work alone?+

Either works. Teams of up to four are encouraged, and students may also work on their own. Students can regroup at the end of a term.

What if we miss a session or two?+

Milestones are assessed on the work, not on attendance. A missed week does not reset progress; the next check-in covers more ground.

Does this count for IB CAS hours?+

A service-oriented project can qualify. Please check with your IB coordinator; we are glad to help document the work.
