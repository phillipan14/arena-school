Questions

A few things families ask.

My child has a busy schedule already. Does this fit?+

Check-ins are 25 minutes a week, in person or online, and the async updates take a few minutes. It's built to sit alongside exams, sports, and everything else, not to compete with them.

Does my child need a team, or can they go solo?+

Teams of up to four are encouraged, and solo founders are a legitimate company, not a lesser one. Recombination happens naturally at term boundaries: think of it as hiring, students see each other's work and decide who to build with next.

What if we miss a session or two?+

Milestones are judged by proof, not attendance. The program is drop-in tolerant: a missed week doesn't reset the ladder, it just means the next check-in has more to cover.

Does this count for IB CAS hours?+

A service-oriented project can qualify for CAS hours. Talk to your IB coordinator, and we're glad to help document the work.

How do we join?+

Arena alumni join directly. New students start with a starter intensive, the next one is the November long weekend, then move straight into the School-Year Mentorship Program. Request a spot with the form below.

September to June, one company gets built. By the end, there's proof no application can fake.
