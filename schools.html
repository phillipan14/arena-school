Four ways to run it

Pick the format that fits your calendar. Every one ends with something real, shipped.

Programs are designed around a school year, not against it: breaks for intensives, term time for mentorship, and teacher enablement so the capability compounds inside your school.

The five-day intensive: the flagship.

Over a school break, teams go from blank page to a live product with real users, a brand, an advertisement, a price, and first revenue. Closes with a pitch competition judged by real founders and operators, and a parents' showcase your community will talk about.

School holidays · 15 to 25 students · demo day with real judges and a real prize

The starter intensive: basics in two to three days.

A long weekend or short break: students learn to build with AI, ship a first version, and leave with a working product and the toolkit to keep going. The on-ramp for younger students and first-timers, and the feeder into the school-year program.

Long weekends and short breaks · beginner friendly · the on-ramp format

The school-year program: the company keeps running.

After an intensive, products stay live and companies keep going: mentorship check-ins every two weeks, one verifiable milestone at a time, regular demo days. Light enough to survive exam season, real enough that by June a student has proof no application can fake.

Biweekly check-ins · after school and weekends · fits around exams

Teacher enablement: the capability stays.

Your teachers co-run the cohort with us and keep the playbooks: the day-by-day curriculum, the prompt vault, the project rubrics, the demo-day format. The second cohort is yours to run; we stay on call.

Co-teaching model · playbooks included · curriculum stays with your team

How a partnership works

You bring the room and the students. We bring everything else.

Four steps from first call to demo day, typically eight to twelve weeks ahead of the program dates.

1. Scope: one call.

Your calendar, your students, your goals. We pick the format together and set the dates.

2. Adapt: your students, your context.

We tailor the curriculum to age range, language, and what your community cares about. Recruiting materials and parent communications included.

3. Run: co-delivered on your campus.

We teach and mentor; your teachers co-run and learn the system. The week ends with a public demo day: real judges, real prize, parents in the room.

4. Handoff: the playbooks stay.

Curriculum, prompt vault, rubrics, and demo-day format stay with your team. Follow-on mentorship for students who want to keep building.
