How a partnership works

You bring the students and the space. We bring the program and build it with your team.

Four steps from first call to demo day, usually starting eight to twelve weeks before the program dates.

1. Scope: one call.

Your calendar, your students, your goals. We choose the format together and set the dates.

2. Adapt: your students, your context.

We tailor the curriculum to age, language and what your community cares about, with recruiting materials and parent letters included.

3. Run: co-delivered on your campus.

We lead the build sessions and mentor teams while your teachers co-run the week. It ends in a public demo day with parents in the room.

4. Handoff: the playbooks stay.

The curriculum, prompt vault, rubrics and demo-day format stay with your team.
