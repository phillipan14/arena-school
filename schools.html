How a partnership works

You bring the students. We build it with your team.

Four steps from first call to demo day, usually starting eight to twelve weeks before the program dates.

  1. 1Scope

    One call

    Your calendar and goals. Together we choose the format and set the dates.

  2. 2Adapt

    Your context

    We tailor the plan to age and language, with parent letters included.

  3. 3Run

    On your campus

    We lead the sessions with your teachers, ending with families in the room.

  4. 4Hand off

    The playbook stays

    The curriculum, rubrics, and demo-day format stay with your team.

Fair questions

What school leaders ask.

What does the school provide?+

A room, a laptop for each student, and a teacher who co-runs the sessions with us. We bring the plan and the materials.

Which AI tools do students use?+

Whatever your school approves, including tools that work in China. The skill is judging the result, so it isn’t tied to one product.

How is it priced?+

Per cohort, depending on group size, length, and format. Tell us about your school and we’ll share options.
