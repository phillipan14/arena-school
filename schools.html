Fair questions

What school leaders ask.

We have no time for more PD.+

Start small: one live session at a staff meeting you already hold. Student programs can run outside school time.

Will it fit our mission and values?+

The scoping call starts from your goals and values, and the plan is shaped around them.

Is this a gimmicky AI class?+

No. Students practice agency, taste, and craft by making real work and finishing it. Our approach →

What about tools and child protection?+

We use only the tools your school approves and keep student data out of them. For technology teams →

What does the school provide?+

A room, a laptop for each student, and a teacher who co-runs the sessions. We bring the lesson plan, the materials, and a setup that works with your approved tools.

How is it priced?+

Per cohort, based on group size, length, and format. Send grades, dates, and numbers; we reply within three working days.
