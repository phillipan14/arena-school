How a partnership works

Planned with your team, run on your campus.

Four steps, usually starting eight to twelve weeks before the program dates.

Fair questions

What school leaders ask.

What does the school provide?+

A room, a laptop for each student, and a teacher who co-runs the sessions with us. We bring the lesson plan, the materials, and a setup that works with your approved tools.

Which AI tools do students use?+

Whichever tools your school approves, including ones that work in China. We teach a method, directing the tools and judging the result, so it carries over to whatever comes next.

How is it priced?+

Per cohort, based on group size, length, and format. Share your grades, dates, and student numbers; we reply within three working days.
