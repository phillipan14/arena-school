How a partnership works

You bring the room and the students. We bring everything else.

Four steps from first call to demo day, typically eight to twelve weeks ahead of the program dates.

1. Scope: one call.

Your calendar, your students, your goals. We pick the format together and set the dates.

2. Adapt: your students, your context.

We tailor the curriculum to age range, language, and what your community cares about. Recruiting materials and parent communications included.

3. Run: co-delivered on your campus.

We teach and mentor; your teachers co-run and learn the system. The week ends with a public demo day: real judges, real prize, parents in the room.

4. Handoff: the playbooks stay.

Curriculum, prompt vault, rubrics, and demo-day format stay with your team. Follow-on mentorship for students who want to keep building.
