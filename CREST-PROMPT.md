# Arena School — Crest Generation Prompt

*Paste into ChatGPT (with image gen), Midjourney, Ideogram, or any image tool. Works as-is. Midjourney users: append `--ar 1:1 --style raw --v 6` for cleaner vector-feel output.*

---

Generate a circular academic seal/crest for a new school called **Arena**, the world's first AI-native school. Single color throughout: warm saffron-rust **#C45D35**. Background: warm cream paper **#F4EFE3**. Clean vector style — like a university crest or letterpress mark, not a photo.

## Required text elements

- Outer ring with the wordmark **ARENA** centered along the top, set in tracked Geist Mono or similar geometric monospace capitals with small dot separators between each letter: `A · R · E · N · A`
- Outer ring with the institutional line `A SCHOOL FOR THE AI ERA · EST · MMXXVI` wrapping along the bottom of the ring
- A small italic Newsreader serif motto centered beneath the figure: `IN ARENA`

## Pick ONE of three central-figure directions

**1. Roman arch.** A single, iconic Roman triumphal arch viewed face-on, drawn in clean single-weight line. Three small saffron stars (five-point, hand-drawn feel) arrayed above the arch keystone — one at apex, two at shoulders — representing the school's three rotating campuses (San Francisco, Shenzhen, Bali). The arch references both the literal "arena" (the Colosseum gate) and Theodore Roosevelt's *"Man in the Arena"* speech. Classical, not skeuomorphic — strokes, not stonework detail.

**2. Torch and laurel.** A vertical flaming torch in the center, framed by a thin half-laurel wreath that opens toward the top. Three small filled-circle markers break the wreath at top, lower-left, and lower-right — marking the three campuses. Symbolizes classical virtue, knowledge passed forward, and the three-city rotation.

**3. Geometric A-monogram.** A large capital letter **A** rendered as a clean isosceles triangle outline (no horizontal bar) inside the ring. Three small filled saffron circles sit at the triangle's three vertices — top (SF), lower-right (Shenzhen), lower-left (Bali). A single small dot at the geometric center of the triangle — "the arena." Subtle, geometric, and reads at favicon size.

## Visual style — must follow

- **Single-color saffron on cream.** No gradients, no shadows, no textures, no fills inside outlines.
- **Single-weight stroke work**, roughly 1.5–2pt for the outer ring, 2–3pt for the central figure. Hairline differences acceptable for hierarchy.
- **Centered, symmetrical, generous breathing room** between the outer ring and the central figure.
- **Readable at two sizes**: 32 × 32 favicon AND 400 × 400 hero placement.
- **Type pairing**: Geist Mono tracked caps for ring text; Newsreader italic serif for the motto only.
- **Mood reference**: Belmont High School seal, Phillips Exeter Academy crest, Phaidon book marques, Aesop store signage. Classical / editorial / restrained.

## Explicitly avoid

- **Pareidolia.** No composition that reads as a face (e.g., dots above arcs that look like eyes and a mouth — the current iteration has this problem).
- **Sound-wave / wifi / radar imagery.** No concentric arcs spreading from a center dot.
- **Heraldic clichés.** No crowns, no shields, no banners, no scrolls, no draped flags.
- **Realistic illustration.** No detailed Colosseum stonework, no rendered flame texture on the torch — keep everything stroke-based.
- **Tech-sector clichés.** No chip patterns, no network nodes, no abstract circuitry, no brackets, no slashes.
- **More than two type weights or two font sizes inside the ring.**
- **Multi-color, multi-tone, or photographic treatment.** Saffron-on-cream only.
- **Soft halos or glows on the hub-dots.** The current iteration has these — they make the dots feel cartoonish. Use a single hard-edged dot.

## Output

Generate **three options, one per central-figure direction.** Each as a 1024 × 1024 PNG with transparent background. If the tool supports SVG export, include the vector version — I will use it directly in a website.
