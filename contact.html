Write to us.

About two minutes.

What happens next

  1. 01
    A reply within three working days. By email.
  2. 02
    A short call, if useful. About your school, child, or event.
  3. 03
    Options in writing. Programs, dates, and next steps.

We use your details only to reply, and never share them.

Questions

Before you write.

How fast will you reply?

Within three working days, by email.

Do programs run at our school?

Yes. School programs run on your campus, planned around your calendar.

Which languages?

English or Chinese.

How is a school program priced?

Per cohort, based on group size, length, and format. Send grades, dates, and numbers.

Can families enroll directly?

Yes, in the Year-Long Project Mentorship, which starts on a rolling basis.
