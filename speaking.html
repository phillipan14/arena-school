Session menu

Six sessions, ready to place in a program.

75 minutes

Teaching with AI: get your week back

The hours teachers lose every week to grading, differentiation, and reporting, and what happens to them when AI does the parts that were never teaching. Built from real cases teachers bring to the session, with live demonstrations on their own materials. Participants leave with two or three working tools for their own classes.

All subjects · no technical background needed · hands on keyboards

75 minutes

Assessment after AI

Students now have the same tools teachers do. This session works through what still measures thinking: task design that survives AI assistance, how to assess process rather than product, and what to stop asking for. Practical, with examples rebuilt live from participants' own assignments.

All subjects · especially useful for department heads and curriculum leads

75 minutes

What happens when students build real companies in five days

A case study from an AI Founders program run at an international school: how students went from a blank page to live products with real users in a single week, what the teaching actually looked like day by day, and which parts transfer into a normal classroom or after-school program.

For teachers, heads of school, and anyone designing a program

75 minutes

Build a classroom tool in an hour, no code

A working session. Each participant describes one thing they wish existed for their class, a simulation, a practice generator, a feedback assistant, and builds it during the session. No coding experience required. Everyone leaves with something that runs.

Hands-on · laptops required · caps around 25 for quality

Full day, 6 hours

The AI-fluent teacher: a working day

The pre-conference format. Morning covers the core workflows: feedback at depth, planning and materials, communication with families. Afternoon is building: each teacher creates tools for their own classes with support in the room. We collect participants' most time-consuming tasks beforehand so the day runs on their real work, not demo data.

3 hours + 3 hours · around 15 to 25 teachers · the most productive format

60 to 90 minutes

AI, careers, and college admissions: a session for parents

The conversation schools are being asked for by families: how hiring and admissions are actually changing, what still makes a student stand out, and what parents can do at each age. Delivered calmly, without hype in either direction, in English or Chinese.

Evening or morning parent event · bilingual delivery available

Practical details

What organizers usually ask.

Group size
Sessions scale to any room. Hands-on formats work best at about 25 people or fewer.

What the room needs
Wifi, a projector, and laptops for participants in the hands-on sessions. Nothing installed in advance.

Languages
English or Chinese, with bilingual materials for international school communities.

Preparation
We ask participants one question beforehand: what takes the most time in your week? The session is built from their answers.

Travel
Based between Asia and the United States, and regularly in Beijing, Hong Kong, and Southeast Asia.

Booking
Write with your dates and audience, and we will send session options that fit.
