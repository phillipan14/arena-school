With AI, a first version of almost anything comes together faster. Knowing what to build, and finishing it, sets a new hire apart.

In one hands-on week, participants learn how a product is made, how to use AI tools in real work, and how to keep building on their own.

Outcomes

What participants leave with.

Built for internship and job applications, and for a first role where AI is part of the work.

  • 01A live AI product

    Launched during the week, with real users, and still running afterward.

  • 02A role-specific project

    A tool built for the role each participant is applying to.

  • 03A practiced presentation

    How to explain what they built, practiced on stage at demo day.

  • 04A portfolio

    Code on GitHub and a personal website that presents the work.

The week

Seven days, from problem to demo day.

Pre-work arrives before day one. Two company visits and guest firesides run across the build days.

  1. Day 01Problem selection

    Feedback on pre-work, then each participant commits to a problem.

  2. Day 02User research and PRD

    Interviews with users and a mini-PRD with prioritized features.

  3. Day 03Build with AI coding tools

    Participants build with AI coding tools and make every decision.

  4. Day 04Brand, landing page, ad

    A name, a design system, a landing page, and an advertisement.

  5. Day 05Launch, pricing, build freeze

    The product goes live, gets priced, and finds its first users.

  6. Day 06Story workshop and target-job build

    Pitch and interview framing, the target-job build, and a full rehearsal.

  7. Day 07Demo day

    A live pitch to founders and operators, with awards.

ApplyRequest an invitation with your school or current role. Enrollment opens in October.
Do I need to know how to code?+

No coding experience is needed. Participants build with AI coding tools, learn how their product works, and make every decision along the way, which is how many teams now build at work.

What if I already have a job offer?+

It is a good time to attend. Participants start the new role having already built and launched a product with AI, with a portfolio and a practiced way to explain their work.

What do I need to bring?+

A laptop and a problem you care about. Pre-work arrives before day one, so you start the week ready to commit to that problem.
