Why it matters

Walk into your first interview with something to show.

For college and masters students applying to internships and first jobs, and for new hires who want to contribute from day one.

  • 01A live AI product

    With users, launched during the week and still running after it.

  • 02A target-job build

    A tool you build for the exact role you are applying to.

  • 03A rehearsed story

    How you explain what you built, practiced on a live stage.

  • 04A portfolio

    Code on GitHub and a personal site that presents the work.

The week

Seven days, from problem to demo day.

Pre-work arrives before day one. Two company visits and guest firesides run across the build days.

  1. Day 01
    Problem selection

    Feedback on your pre-work, then you commit to a problem.

  2. Day 02
    User research and PRD

    Interviews with users and a mini-PRD with prioritized features.

  3. Day 03
    Build with AI coding tools

    You build the product with AI coding tools and make the calls.

  4. Day 04
    Brand, landing page, ad

    A name, a design system, a landing page and an advertisement.

  5. Day 05
    Launch, pricing, build freeze

    The product goes live, gets priced and finds its first users.

  6. Day 06
    Story workshop and target-job build

    Pitch and interview framing, the target-job build, a full rehearsal.

  7. Day 07
    Demo day

    A live pitch to founders and operators, with awards.
