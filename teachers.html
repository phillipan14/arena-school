Teaching with AI: get your week back.

Practical, hands-on training for teachers of any subject and any comfort level. No coding. Bring the work you actually do, and leave with tools that work in your classroom on Monday.

The premise

AI makes teachers more important, not less.

The parts of the job that AI does well were never the teaching: the second set of comments on the same essay, the worksheet retyped at three levels, the report card sentences written forty times. Hand those over and what is left is the work only a person can do. That is the whole point of this training.

What we cover

Real cases, not a tour of tools.

Every session is built from cases teachers brought us: the hours actually being spent, and what happens to them.

01

Feedback with depth, at speed

Give every student specific, personal feedback on their writing or problem sets without losing your evenings to it.

02

Planning and materials

Unit plans, differentiated worksheets, reading sets at three levels, and rubrics that match how you actually grade.

03

Build a simulation, no coding

Watch a working interactive get built from a plain description. Science, math, music theory, anything you can explain.

04

Assessment after AI

Design tasks that still measure thinking when students have the same tools you do.

05

Families and communication

Reports, parent notes, and translations for multilingual families, in your voice rather than a template.

06

Your own workflow, live

Bring the recurring task that eats your month. We automate it together, in the room, and you leave with it working.

How it runs

Three formats, all hands on keyboards.

Before every session we ask participants one question: what takes the most time in your week? The session is then built around their answers, so the examples on screen are their own.

Full-day workshop

Six hours, hands-on. Every teacher leaves with two or three working tools built for their own classes.

6 hours · up to about 25 teachers

Single session

Seventy-five minutes on one high-value workflow, demonstrated live and rebuilt by participants.

75 minutes · conference or staff meeting format

School-wide faculty training

Tailored by department, delivered across a professional development day, with follow-up support for the teachers who want to go further.

Half day to two days · whole faculty
