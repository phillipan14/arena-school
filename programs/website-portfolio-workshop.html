Why a portfolio

Anyone can now write a polished essay. A portfolio shows the work behind it.

A portfolio shows the work itself, not a description of it. Building one hands-on, students learn how a website is made, how to use the new tools, and how to present their work wherever it matters.

Questions

Common questions

Do students need to know how to code?+

No coding experience is needed. Students describe what they want in plain language, AI coding tools draft it, and students test the result and decide what stays. Directing the tools and judging what they produce is the skill we teach.

Is the portfolio the student’s own work?+

Yes. Students choose what to include, write every word, and make the design decisions. AI tools help them build faster and try more layouts; what the site says and shows is theirs.

Where does the link go in an application?+

Many applications have room for a link, often in the additional information section. The same site also serves summer program, scholarship, and internship applications. Your counselors advise on what each college accepts.

Will a portfolio improve admission chances?+

No one can promise an admissions outcome, and we do not. A portfolio lets readers see the student’s actual work rather than a description of it. Each college makes its own decision.

How much does it cost?+

We set it with each school, based on group size, length, and format. Tell us your grades, dates, and number of students, and we will reply within three working days with options.

What does the school provide?+

A room, a laptop for each student, and a teacher or counselor who co-runs the days with us. We bring the lesson plan, the materials, and a setup that works with your approved tools.
