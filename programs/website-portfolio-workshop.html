How it runs

Day one, the portfolio goes live. Day two, students make it their own.

  1. Day 1 · morning
    Choose the story Agency

    Students decide what to show and in what order, before any design.

  2. Day 1 · afternoon
    Build and go live Taste

    They describe each page to AI tools, judge what comes back, and publish by the end of the day.

  3. Day 2
    Improve and publish Craft

    They add a demo or simulation, refine the design, get feedback from classmates, and publish again.

Fair questions

What students, parents, and schools ask.

Do students need to know how to code?+

No. Students describe what they want in plain language, check what the AI tools produce, and decide what stays.

Is the portfolio really the student’s own work?+

Yes. Students choose the content, write the words, and make every design decision. AI tools help them try more versions and test sooner; the story and the judgment are theirs.

Where does the link go in a college application?+

Many applications have room for links, such as the additional information section and some supplements. Your school’s counselors advise on each college.

What if a student doesn’t have much work yet?+

The demo they build on day two becomes their first piece. Projects from class, clubs, and competitions all count.

Which AI tools do students use?+

Whatever your school approves, including tools that work in China. The skill is describing what they want and judging the result, so it isn’t tied to one product.

How much does it cost?+

It depends on group size, length, and format, which we shape with each school. Tell us about yours and we’ll share options.

What does the school provide?+

A room, a laptop for each student, and a teacher who co-runs the days with us. We bring the plan and the materials.
