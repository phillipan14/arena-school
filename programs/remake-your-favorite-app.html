Why it matters

Students use apps every day. Few know how one is made.

The course starts from software students already know and uses it to teach how products are designed, built, and improved.

  • How software is designed

    Taking apart a familiar app shows why each screen, button, and step is there.

  • Practical AI skills

    Students learn to describe what they want to AI coding tools, test the result, and fix what does not work.

  • From using to making

    Each student leaves with an app of their own, published at a link they can share.

Questions

Common questions

Do students need to know how to code?+

No. Students describe what they want in plain language, check what the AI tools produce, and decide what stays.

Is it just a copy of the real app?+

No. Students rebuild the core idea to learn how it works, then give it a new name, their own design, and a new feature.

Won’t this mean more screen time?+

Students plan on paper, test with classmates, and spend their screen time making something rather than scrolling.

Which AI tools do students use?+

Whatever your school approves, including tools that work in China.

How much does it cost?+

It depends on group size, length, and format. Tell us about your school and we will share options.

What does the school provide?+

A room, a laptop for each student, and a teacher who co-runs the days with us. We bring the plan and the materials.
