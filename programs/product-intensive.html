Why it matters

With AI’s help, more people than ever can build a product. This week is for practicing what to choose, how to judge it, and how to finish it.

In one hands-on week, students learn how a product is made from problem to launch, how to use AI tools across the work, and how to start the next project on their own.

Questions

Common questions

Do students need to know how to code?+

No coding experience is needed. Students describe what they want in plain language, AI coding tools draft it, and students test the result and decide what stays. Directing the tools and judging what they produce is the skill we teach.

Who judges demo day?+

A panel of founders and operators who have built real products. In Beijing, judges came from Harvard, Stanford, Y Combinator, and AI labs, and tested each team’s product before the pitch was scored.

How are teams formed?+

On day one, students pitch problems they care about, and teams form around them. Each student chooses the team they join, and the team splits roles across research, building, and design.

Which AI tools do students use?+

Whichever tools your school approves, including ones that work in China. We teach a method rather than one product, so students can apply it to whatever tools they use next, at school or at home.

How much does it cost?+

We set it with each school, based on group size, length, and format. Tell us your grades, dates, and number of students, and we will reply within three working days with options.
