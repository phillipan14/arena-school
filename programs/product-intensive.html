Fair questions

What schools and parents ask.

Do students need to know how to code?+

No. Students describe what they want in plain language, test what the AI tools produce, and decide what stays.

How are teams formed?+

On day one, students pitch problems for three minutes each, with no slides. Teams form around the problems they care about most.

Who judges demo day?+

A panel of founders and operators. In Beijing, the final pitch was judged by founders and operators from Harvard, Y Combinator, and AI labs.

What do our teachers do?+

They co‑run every day with us, sit with teams while they build, and keep the format to run again.

Which AI tools do students use?+

Whatever your school approves, including tools that work in China. The skill is describing what you want and judging the result.

Is there a shorter version?+

Yes. A two-to-three-day starter covers choosing a problem, building, and a short demo.
