What attendees leave with

More than notes from a talk.

Work from their own classes

Attendees bring a lesson, a rubric, or a letter home and work on it with AI in the room. The choices stay theirs.

Prompts and templates to keep

Time back

Hands-on. Teachers build something they use the next week.

  • Report card weekend, in two hoursDrafts written in each teacher’s own voice from their past comments.
  • One lesson, five levelsThe same text for English learners, learning support, on-level, and advanced readers, with the same goal.
  • Exit tickets at 3pm, reteach groups by 3:05Misconceptions grouped and short reteach plans ready before the next class.

Teaching in the AI age

For teachers weighing how AI fits their assessment.

  • Your assignment stopped workingRebuild one assignment so that using AI becomes part of the learning, with reasoning assessed.
  • Design for the inevitableA framework for what to assess, what to let AI do, and what to make visible, applied to a real unit.
  • The cheating conversation, retiredGrading the process: oral defenses, visible drafts, and AI transcripts as part of the submission.
