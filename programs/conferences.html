What we share

Sessions educators can use the next school day.

Every session is hands-on and built on attendees’ own subjects and materials, so educators learn how the tools work and leave ready to design project-based work with them.

  • Hands-on practice

    Attendees use AI on their own lessons and materials, with help in the room.

  • Examples from real work

    Drawn from our staff training at Tsinghua University and our student programs in Hong Kong and Beijing.

  • Balanced guidance

    Where AI helps, where it falls short, and how teachers keep decisions about teaching and assessment.

Formats

Four formats, each shaped with the organizer.

At the conference

Keynote or talk

How AI is changing teaching, learning, and work, with a live demo.

At the conference

Breakout workshop

Laptops open, no coding. Attendees work on their own material with help in the room.

At the conference

Panel

A member of our team joins your panel on AI in schools.

Before the conference

Pre-conference workshop

One or two hands-on days. Educators leave with materials for their own classes.

Session topics

Three tracks to choose from.

Classroom practice

Hands-on, for any subject.

  • Report comments in each teacher’s voiceDrafts based on their past comments, which the teacher edits and approves.
  • One text, several reading levelsThe same text for English learners, learning support, on-level, and advanced readers.
  • From exit tickets to reteach groupsSorting common misconceptions and planning a short reteach.

Teaching and assessment

For teachers deciding how AI fits their assessment.

  • Redesigning an assignmentMaking AI use part of the learning, with reasoning assessed.
  • Deciding what to assessA framework applied to a real unit.
  • Assessing the processOral defenses, visible drafts, and AI transcripts in the submission.

Leadership and parents

A talk and open conversation.

  • Working Beside AI: What It Means for Our Kids’ FuturesHow work is changing and what students can already make, followed by questions.

Questions

Common questions

How is a session planned?+

We agree the topic, audience, and length with you, then build the examples around your attendees’ subjects, so each educator works on material they already teach.

Which AI tools do attendees use?+

Tools that suit your audience, including ones that work in China. We teach a method rather than one product, so it carries over to whatever tools attendees use next.

How much does it cost?+

It depends on the format and length. Tell us about your event, audience, and dates, and we will reply within three working days with options.
