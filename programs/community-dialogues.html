Questions

Common questions

Who should attend?+

Parents of middle and high school students, parent associations, and school leaders and boards. We fit the examples to the ages of the students whose families are in the room.

Is this a presentation about Arena’s programs?+

No. The session shares what we have seen of AI at work, in hiring, and in admissions, and what students can do with it. We do not present programs or pricing.

How long is a session?+

We set the length with each school, from a morning parent coffee to an evening talk or a longer discussion with school leaders.

Can it be in Chinese?+

Yes. Dialogues run in English or Chinese.
