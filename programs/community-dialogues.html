Parents of middle and high school students, boards and heads of school, and advisors or consultants who work with families.

Yes. We run sessions in English or Chinese, with bilingual slides.

Do you tell families what to do?+

We share what we see and offer ideas for each age. The decisions stay with your families and your school.

How long is a session?+

We set the length with each school, from a short parent evening to a longer board briefing.
