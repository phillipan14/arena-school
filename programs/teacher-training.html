How we work with teachers

We start from what already works.

We don’t redesign your course. Each session starts from one small part of one class.

  1. 01 · Before

    Which tool do you wish existed?

    Each teacher brings one task from a class they teach now.

  2. 02 · Show

    One tool, live

    We make one tool on screen from a plain-language description.

  3. 03 · Make

    Your own tool

    Each teacher makes theirs on your approved tools and checks every result.

  4. 04 · Keep

    Ready for class

    Teachers keep the tool and the prompt behind it, to reuse and share.

Decisions about teaching and assessment stay with your faculty.

  • 01 · A staff meeting

    Live session

    We show one task live, then teachers try it on their own material.

  • 02 · A department

    Hands-on workshop

    Teachers work through several tasks on their own materials, with help in the room.

  • 03 · The whole faculty

    School-wide training

    Each department gets a session, then a follow-up.

Questions

Common questions.

Is this more work for me?

It starts from one task you already do, and the tool is made during the session. You decide whether to keep using it.

I’m not technical. Will I keep up?

Yes. No technical background or coding is needed.

What about students using AI to cheat?

We address it directly: how to assess the process, through drafts, reflections, and the reasons behind each change, and when AI doesn’t belong.

Our school restricts tools and student data.

We work only on the tools your school approves, and agree the data rules with your technology team first. For technology staff →
