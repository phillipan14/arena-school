Fair questions

What teachers usually ask.

I’m not technical. Will I keep up?+

The sessions assume nothing, and there is no coding. If you can explain a task to a student teacher, you can do this.

My students use AI to cheat. Doesn’t this make it worse?+

It’s a real concern. We cover assessment design for a world where students have AI too, so you can decide how the tools fit in your class, or whether they do.

Our school restricts which tools we can use.+

We adapt to whatever your school approves, including tools that work in China, such as Kimi, Doubao, and DeepSeek.

Will AI make my feedback feel generic?+

Your feedback stays yours. AI can help you spot patterns or check a comment against your rubric; what each student hears is your call.
