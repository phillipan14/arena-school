How it runs

One session, four steps.

  1. 01 · Before

    One question

    Which tasks take up most of your week?

  2. 02 · Show

    One task, live

    A task most of the room shares, worked through together.

  3. 03 · Practice

    On your own class

    Each teacher works on their own materials and reviews every result.

  4. 04 · Keep

    Shared templates

    Departments keep prompts and templates to reuse and adapt.

Sessions use the AI tools your school approves. Decisions about teaching and assessment stay with your faculty.

Questions

Common questions

I’m not technical. Will I keep up?+

Yes. The sessions assume no technical background, and there is no coding.

What about students using AI to cheat?+

It is a real concern. We cover assessment design for classes where students also have AI, so you decide how the tools fit, or whether they do.

Our school restricts which tools we can use.+

We adapt to whatever your school approves, including tools that work in China, such as Kimi, Doubao, and DeepSeek.

How long is the training?+

Length is set with each school, from a single session to school-wide training with follow-up.
