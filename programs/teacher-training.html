Why it matters

AI can draft almost anything now. Deciding what belongs in the classroom is still the teacher’s call.

Most teachers can already reach AI tools. Working hands-on with their own lessons, teachers learn how the tools work, when they help, and how to keep making their own materials.

Questions

Common questions

I’m not technical. Will I keep up?+

Yes. The sessions assume no technical background and involve no coding. Teachers work on their own lessons and materials, so every step connects to something they already teach.

What about students using AI to cheat?+

It is a real concern, and we address it directly. We cover assessment design for classes where students also have AI: which tasks to redesign, what to check, and how to decide whether the tools belong at all.

Our school restricts which tools we can use.+

We adapt to whatever your school approves, including tools that work in China, such as Kimi, Doubao, and DeepSeek. We teach a method rather than one product, so it carries over to whatever you use next.

How long is the training?+

We set it with each school, from a single hands-on session to school-wide training with follow-up. Every format ends with materials teachers made themselves.
