Fair questions

What teachers usually ask.

I’m not technical. Will I keep up?+

The sessions assume nothing, and there is no coding. If you can explain a task to a student teacher, you can do this. Teachers who arrive skeptical often leave with the most.

My students use AI to cheat. Doesn’t this make it worse?+

It’s a real concern. We cover assessment design for a world where students have AI too, so you can decide with full information how the tools fit in your class, or whether they do.

Our school restricts which tools we can use.+

We adapt to whatever your school approves, including tools that work in China, such as Kimi, Doubao and DeepSeek. The skill is describing your task precisely; it isn’t tied to one product.

Will AI make my feedback feel generic?+

Your feedback stays yours. AI can help you spot patterns across a class or check a comment against your rubric; what each student hears, and in what voice, is your call.
