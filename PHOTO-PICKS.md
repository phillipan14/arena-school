# Hero photo picks (cine4 / cine5, 2026-10-16)

Every real photo in `photos/**` (stock excluded) was opened and judged for brightness, energy, and faces: smiling, engaged, presenting, hands-on, good lighting. These run in the full-bleed hero crossfade (`cine.js`), first photo loaded eagerly and the rest after it.

## Chosen

| Order | File | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 1 (home) | `photos/isb-2026/pitch-duo` (800 / 1600 webp) | Two students presenting at demo day, one laughing, one smiling with the mic. Warm light, the most joyful frame in the set. Leads the homepage rotation. |
| 2 (home) | `photos/isb-hq/isb-pitch-gesture` (800 / 1400) | A student pitching with a big confident gesture, a teammate at the podium, the audience behind. Energy and ownership. |
| 3 (home), 2 (tech) | `photos/isb-2026/mentor-feedback` (800 / 1600) | Mentors visibly delighted by a student demo. Bright faces, good light; it shows adults reacting to student work. |
| 4 (home), 1 (tech) | `photos/steps/collab-stage-poster` (1024 px) | Hands-on teamwork in a daylight room (Hong Kong 2025): students at laptops, a teammate leaning in. The brightest scene in the repo. A video still, so 1024 px wide; fine under the navy gradient. Leads the Technology rotation. |

Technology also uses `pitch-duo` as its third photo.
