About Arena School

Hands-on programs for students, educators, and families.

Arena School runs hands-on programs where students build real products with AI tools and learn the judgment those tools cannot supply: agency, choosing what is worth solving; taste, telling which version works; and craft, finishing it well. Based in Beijing, we often work on schools’ own campuses, with the AI tools each school approves. Programs, results, and our team are at arenaschool.org.

Our core format: the five-day intensive

Student teams choose a problem worth solving, build a working product with AI tools, and defend it before a panel. One decision a day, and each day ends with something students can show. No coding experience is needed.

1Choose a problemTeams form around problems pitched in three minutes.
2Talk to usersInterviews with the people they want to help.
3Live at a linkA working version anyone can open.
4Brand and priceName, price, and a fixed hour when building stops.
5Demo dayTeams pitch to a panel of founders.
