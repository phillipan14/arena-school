Fair questions

What technology teams ask.

We can’t use frontier models. Does that rule this out?+

No. We build for your school’s requirements and approved systems, such as enterprise Copilot. What teachers learn is a method, so it works on the tools you allow.

Where does student data go?+

Not into AI tools. Teacher sessions use teachers’ own materials, not student records, and we agree the data rules for any student program with your team before it starts.

Which tools do you use?+

Whichever your school approves: enterprise Copilot, tools that work in China such as Kimi, Doubao, and DeepSeek, or others. We review them with you before planning any session.

Who supports it after you leave?+

Your tech coaches, with what stays behind: session plans, teacher examples, and shared templates. Because teachers learn a method rather than one product, there is less to troubleshoot when tools change.
